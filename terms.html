Terms and Conditions

1. Scope of Services

Watch Clinic specializes exclusively in the service, maintenance, repair, and restoration of fine Swiss luxury timepieces, vintage watches, and pocket watches. By entrusting your timepiece to our workshop, you agree to the terms outlined below.

2. Service Estimates and Approvals

  • Inspection: Every timepiece undergoes a comprehensive diagnostic evaluation prior to any repair work. Standard initial inspections are complimentary.
  • Client Consent: All price estimates and repair scopes are communicated to the client for explicit approval before work begins. No unexpected or unauthorized repairs will be conducted.

3. Service Warranty

We take immense pride in our craftsmanship and stand behind the quality of our work. We offer a 1-year service warranty on completed movement overhauls and internal mechanical repairs, governed by the terms below.

  • What is Covered:
    • Internal movement functionality and newly replaced internal mechanical components.
    • Sapphire crystal replacements (against structural failure or installation defects).
    • Steel and metal watch bracelets (against clasp or link mechanism failure).
  • What is NOT Covered:
    • Accidental damage, impact, drops, or mishandling by the client or third parties.
    • Water damage on timepieces with compromised, aged, or soft metal cases where water resistance cannot be guaranteed.

Warranty Limitations & Fine Print

Disclaimer: The 1-year service warranty is strictly limited to internal movement overhauls and mechanical repairs performed by Watch Clinic. The warranty explicitly excludes plexiglass (acrylic crystals) due to their soft material composition and vulnerability to external impacts, all leather, rubber, and fabric watch straps, and exterior polishing. Because precious metals and polished finishes can scratch easily from normal daily use, subsequent micro-scratches and cosmetic wear are not covered. Furthermore, water resistance cannot be guaranteed or warranted for vintage timepieces or models with worn or soft case structures.
